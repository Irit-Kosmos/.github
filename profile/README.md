## IRIT & Kosmos Collaboration

IRIT (Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse) is the Toulouse Institute of Computer Science Research.
Kosmos Education is a software publishing company specialising in the education sector.
IRIT and Kosmos have been collaborating for several years on research projects in the field of Technology-Enhanced Learning (TEL).
The GitHub repositories hosted in this space are open-source software outputs from this collaboration.
